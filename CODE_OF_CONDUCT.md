# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include using
welcoming language, respecting differing viewpoints, accepting constructive
feedback, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, trolling, public or private abuse,
publishing others' private information, and other conduct that would be
considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the repository maintainers through the contact
method listed in the repository. Reports will be reviewed and handled fairly
and confidentially where possible.
